Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Press and hold anywhere to start a dive. Keep your finger on the screen to steer — the spark follows with a little inertia, so lead it ahead. Eat glowing plankton to raise your combo, and pass close to the dark spines ("grazing") for bonus points. Touching a spine ends the run. On the game-over screen, tap anywhere to dive again.

What are pearls and trails?

Pearls are an in-game currency you earn only by playing: from your runs, clean boss gates, Daily Tides missions and the Daily Gift. The colored dots on the title screen are trail colors. Most unlock when you reach a score, or can be unlocked early with pearls (tap a locked dot twice to confirm); Pearl and Aurora are pearl-only. Pearls cannot be bought with real money.

What is the Daily Dive?

Once per day everyone gets the same seeded dive. Your best Daily Dive score goes to its own Game Center leaderboard. Open it from Settings → Daily Dive.

Why do I see ads?

Lumen Drift is free to play. An occasional full-screen ad may appear between runs (never during a run). Other ads are optional: they appear only when you tap a reward button such as Revive, Start with WARD, Double pearls or Claim ×2.

What does Remove Ads do?

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase in Settings. It permanently removes the between-run ads. Optional reward buttons stay available, since you only see those ads when you choose to.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchase. You must be signed in with the same Apple ID that made the original purchase.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where required by law (for example in the EU, UK and Switzerland), a consent form appears at first launch. You can review it later in Settings → Ad privacy. You can also turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Game Center achievements and leaderboards

Lumen Drift has 11 achievements and 2 leaderboards (All-Time Best and Daily Dive). Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings, then open them from Settings → Leaderboards or Achievements.

Where is my progress saved?

Your best scores, pearls, trails and settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes this local data. Scores and achievements you have earned remain in Game Center.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Open Settings (the gear icon). There are separate toggles for music, sound effects and haptics.

The game crashed / something looks broken

Please email us with a screenshot or short description of what happened, your iPhone or iPad model, and your iOS version. We do our best to fix issues quickly.

What happens with my data?

See our Privacy Policy. In short: there are no accounts, your game data stays on your device, and the only third party that receives data is Google AdMob, for showing ads.
