LUMEN DRIFT

Eat the light. Graze the dark. A one-finger deep-sea arcade game.

About

Steer a glowing spark up a pitch-black ocean trench. Eat plankton to build your combo, slip past dark spines for big bonus points, and dive through five depth zones, each with its own hazards and a boss gate at the end. Runs last 30–90 seconds, so there is always time for one more dive.

Play the shared Daily Dive, finish three Daily Tides missions, collect a Daily Gift, unlock eight neon trail colors, and chase your friends on Game Center leaderboards.

Available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
