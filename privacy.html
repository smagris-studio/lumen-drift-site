Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

Lumen Drift: Deep Sea ("the App") is developed and operated by Smagris Studio ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Lumen Drift does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email or precise location, and does not collect personally identifying information directly. The App uses third-party services that may collect limited data, as described below.

1.1 Locally stored data

Your game progress (best scores, pearls, unlocked trails, daily missions and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not have access to it, and it is removed when you delete the App.

1.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

Lumen Drift shows occasional full-screen ads between runs and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch, delivered by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you grant permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Coarse location (country / region) derived from your IP address.
  • Device information such as model, OS version and language.
  • Ad interaction, diagnostic and performance data needed to deliver, measure and protect ads (including crash and performance data from the ad SDK).

This data is used by Google to serve and measure ads, and, with your permission, to personalize them. Learn more at Google's Ads Privacy page and the Google Privacy Policy.

1.3 Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

In regions where it is required (for example the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland), the App shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. Your choice is stored on your device.

1.4 In-app purchases (Apple StoreKit)

The App offers one optional purchase, Remove Ads. The transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive only the result of the purchase (product and whether it succeeded). We never see your payment details.

1.5 Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards and achievements, together with your Game Center alias. This is managed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not store this data on our own servers.

2. Tracking and App Tracking Transparency

The App asks for your permission before tracking via the IDFA. If you decline, ads still appear but are less personalized. You can change this choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

3. Children's Privacy

Lumen Drift is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not run our own servers or analytics. The only data flows outside your device are:

  • To Apple, for App Store purchases and Game Center.
  • To Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform, for consent and ads (see sections 1.2 and 1.3).

5. Your Choices

  • Ad privacy: review or change your consent at any time in the App under Settings → Ad privacy (shown where required).
  • Tracking: disable it in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase permanently removes between-run ads. Optional rewarded ads appear only when you tap a reward button.
  • Delete local data: deleting the App removes all locally stored game data.

6. Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device for as long as the App is installed. Game Center data is retained by Apple under your Apple Account. Data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies. We do not retain user data ourselves.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" at the top of this page.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please email edijs.smagris.dev@gmail.com.
